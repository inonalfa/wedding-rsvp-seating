# Hosted service policy drafts

**Requires legal review. These outlines are neither approved policies nor legal advice. Do not present them as terms or a privacy notice before review.**

## Privacy notice outline

- Identify the service operator, privacy contact, launch jurisdictions and applicable rights.
- Explain which information the couple provides, which is collected by the service (Google account identifier, event and guest list, attendance and seating), and the purpose of each. Explain how the couple should notify their guests before uploading data.
- Explain essential sessions, Google sign-in, Cloudflare storage and abuse protection. List processors and transfer locations after verifying the deployment. No third-party analytics. Clearly identify optional integrations and their separate operators.
- Explain who can see invitations, guest names and seating. Describe the responsibility to safeguard signed links. Explain export, correction, revocation, deletion requests, retention, and the provider's recovery window.
- Specify legal basis, controller and processor roles, request timelines, incident notification, and the treatment of any information about minors. Have counsel verify Israeli and other applicable requirements.

## Terms outline

- Free service with quotas and no SLA. Explain exhaustion, suspension and discontinuation, how to retain a copy and the availability of the open-source self-hosted alternative.
- State permitted use and prohibit unsolicited messaging, abusive activity and uploading information without authorization. Explain complaint and appeal procedures.
- Explain event deletion, optional provider-owned accounts, and risks of distributing guest links or third-party exports.
- Ask counsel to review liability, disputes, jurisdiction, changes to terms, consumer protection and how acceptance is recorded.
